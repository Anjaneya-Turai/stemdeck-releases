# StemDeck for Windows

Split any song into vocals, drums, bass, guitar, piano and more, on your own computer, then remix it in a live
mixer where every part has its own fader, solo and mute.

## Download

**[Download the latest StemDeck for Windows](https://github.com/Anjaneya-Turai/stemdeck-releases/releases/latest)**
(the `StemDeck-Setup-<version>.exe` file under *Assets*).

## What you need

- Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit
- About 8 GB of free disk space
- An internet connection for the first launch (StemDeck downloads its splitting engine once, about 5 GB)
- Recommended: an NVIDIA graphics card (driver 580 or newer). Without one, songs split on the processor, which
  is slower.

## Installing

1. Run `StemDeck-Setup-<version>.exe`.
2. Windows may show **"Windows protected your PC"**: the installer isn't code-signed yet. Click **More info**,
   then **Run anyway**.
3. On first launch StemDeck sets up its engine (a few minutes, with a progress screen). After that it starts in
   seconds.

## Using it

- Drop in a song file (mp3, m4a, wav, flac, ogg, webm and more) or paste a YouTube link, choose a quality and
  press **Split into stems**. The song opens in the mixer when it's ready.
- If YouTube asks to confirm you aren't a bot, click **Sign in to YouTube** in StemDeck and sign in there. A spare
  Google account is the safest choice. Please only split songs you have the right to use.

## Privacy

Everything runs on your computer. Your songs, stems and YouTube sign-in never leave it; StemDeck only connects to
YouTube (for links), to download its engine and models, and to check for updates.

## Updates

StemDeck updates itself: it downloads new versions in the background and asks before restarting.

## Uninstalling

Windows Settings > Apps > StemDeck > Uninstall. You're asked whether to also delete the splitting engine and your
songs.
